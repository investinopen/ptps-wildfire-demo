This is a map that's meant to be printed out for use by small/rural fire departments. It's a proof of concept and hasn't been validated; use at your own risk.

    Driveways & service roads
    Paths & trails

    Edits to water sources, gates, dead ends, one-way streets, and weight limits in OpenStreetMap take up to three days to appear here. The map covers the 50 states, minus the westernmost Aleutians; there are no building risk scores in Alaska or Hawaii.

    Drag a rectangle over the area to print.

    Print an area

    Splits the area into pages of this map, one per landscape sheet, as outlined on the map, with an index page first. Each page is drawn with the layers that are on now. Keep this tab in front while the pages are drawn, since browsers pause drawing in background tabs. It picks up where it left off when you come back.
